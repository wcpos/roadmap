v2.3.0 — Works with your other plugins

### Due date

### Pitch

The plugins your online store already runs, working at the counter: loyalty points, gift cards, subscriptions, email lists and pricing rules.

### Why this release

Most WCPOS stores also run WooCommerce extensions for loyalty, gift cards, subscriptions, marketing and pricing, and today those stop at the shop door. This release makes the POS a first-class client of those plugins: a customer earns and spends points at the till, a gift card is sold and redeemed in person, a subscription is started at the counter, a captured email lands on the same mailing list as an online signup, and a bulk-price or discount rule applies exactly as it does in the cart online.

### Not in this release

- Building WCPOS's own loyalty, gift card or subscription features. The point is to work with the plugins merchants already pay for.
