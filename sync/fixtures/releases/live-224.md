v1.9.0 — Receipts & printing

### Due date

2026-05-15

### Shipped on

2026-05-15

### Pitch

Receipts the way your shop wants them: a template gallery, thermal printers, and more than one design at once.

### Why this release

Almost three months of work on the last step of a sale. A gallery of ready-made templates covers receipts, invoices, quotes, packing slips, gift receipts, kitchen tickets and right-to-left languages; receipts print straight to 58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers; and a store can install several templates side by side and switch between them in settings.
