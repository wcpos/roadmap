# Vendor checkout flows — how popular POS apps move from cart to payment
Date: 2026-09-07. Every claim below is either sourced (URL follows the claim) or marked **UNVERIFIED**.

**Method.** Vendor help-centre articles were fetched and read directly where the site allowed it; where the article's own screenshots were downloadable (Lightspeed, Loyverse, Erply, Toast, Clover developer docs, Zettle App Store/Play listings) they were viewed and the layout claims below come from them, labelled "(vendor screenshot)". Vendor developer docs and, for Odoo, the open-source templates were used where they describe screen structure. Pages that could not be fetched (`clover.com/help` and some `help.sumup.com` articles render only via JavaScript; several Square articles redirect to the support homepage) are quoted from the search index and flagged "(via search index)". No third-party reviews were needed. Help text describes steps and button names far more often than layout, so an unsourced layout cell says UNVERIFIED rather than guessing.

## Patterns observed

1. **In-place column swap is the most common sourced layout on tablet/desktop.** Lightspeed X, Lightspeed R (desktop), Toast and Loyverse all keep the cart/check where it is and turn the product-grid area into the tender UI; the running balance stays on screen. Full-screen takeovers are Square, Clover (a separate Android activity by construction), Odoo and SumUp POS Pro. Centred modals are Zettle iPad (cart rail still visible) and Erply (cart hidden). Nobody uses a side drawer. SumUp's app/POS Lite has no transition at all.

2. **Line items stay visible in most sourced cases** (Lightspeed X, Lightspeed R desktop, Toast, Loyverse, Zettle iPad, SumUp POS Pro); they disappear in Odoo, Clover, Lightspeed R iPad and Erply. Square and Shopify never say. Restaurant products keep items because tender-time splitting is item-based.

3. **The total rides the primary button, then heads the tender pane.** Lightspeed X ("Pay | 1 item | $18.38"), Lightspeed R iPad, Odoo phone (`Pay` with total beneath), Zettle phone ("Charge €25.00"); then Toast "Balance due," Loyverse "Total amount due," Clover "SALE."

4. **Cash entry = keypad or field + suggested amounts, everywhere:** Shopify, Loyverse, Toast, Lightspeed X (modal chips), Lightspeed R (denominations), Odoo (numpad). Exact-amount shortcuts recur: Toast `Fast cash`, Lightspeed R iPad `Cash`, Lightspeed X card icon, SumUp double-tap, Odoo 19 one-click — each justified as skipping the payment screen for speed.

5. **Split tender is a control on the tender screen** (Square `Split Amount`, Loyverse `SPLIT`, Toast `Split evenly`, Shopify `Split payment`, Lightspeed editable amount, R-Series simultaneous fields, Odoo/Erply stacked lines) — except Square for Restaurants (check-level `Split check`) and Zettle (saved-cart "Charge by items").

6. **Post-payment is a receipt screen whose primary action starts the next sale:** Square `New Sale`/"All Done"; Shopify `Done` → home; Loyverse `NEW SALE`; Lightspeed X `Complete sale`; Lightspeed R `New Sale`/`Start New Sale`; Odoo `New Order`; Toast Go `New order`; Erply `F4 New Sale`. Toast and Zettle make `No receipt` the primary option; Toast states why ("most guests opt out"); Square and Clover let merchants skip the screen.

7. **Back is a left-aligned arrow or X — never a swipe.** Square ("X icon or the back arrow"), Lightspeed X (`←`), Lightspeed R (`‹ Back To Sale`), Loyverse (`←`), Clover (`X`, and "Include a Cancel button"), Zettle (`Cancel`/`←`), Erply (`X`/`✗`), Odoo (`Back`), Shopify (`Back to payment options`).

8. **Phone layouts converge:** one pane at a time, cart behind a bottom or badge button (Square `Review sale`, Odoo `Cart`, Zettle cart icon), an extra `Charge` step tablets elide (SumUp says so explicitly), fewer controls (Odoo drops the numpad; Toast Go moves extras to ⋮), simpler browsing (Loyverse list + Favorites). Lightspeed X has no phone product.

9. **Stated principles.** Clover: "minimize the number of steps required, as Clover apps are often used in fast-paced work environments." Square: "streamline," "declutter," "consistent … across all of your POS devices." Odoo 19: "bypass the payment screen for a faster checkout." Shopify: heavyweight flows are "full-screen interfaces"; the payment screen is closed to extensions. Recent redesigns (Square 2024/25, Square for Retail 2023, Toast "new POS," Odoo 19) cut steps *before or around* the tender screen rather than restructure it; Square's removal of its revert option drew complaints about added taps.
