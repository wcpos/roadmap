# WCPOS 2.0 GA: what is still outstanding (swept 2026-11-05)

Swept across 26 wcpos repos (341 open issues, 70 open PRs), the promotion plan
(`docs/releases/2026-11-01-wcpos-2.0-promotion-and-beta-plan.md`, Phases 5–6), the beta 1 and
beta 2 run records, `docs/releases/2026-10-13-wcpos-2.0-provider-requests-checklist.md`, the
`1.11.x` workflow files in free, Pro and electron, and Sentry Discover for `wcpos-app@2.0.0` and
`WCPOS@2.0.0-beta.*`. Sorted into what the Mac mini can run unattended and what needs Paul.

**State going in.** Beta 2 is out (web + desktop only). Sentry on the 2.0 app: 2 events, 1 user,
nothing new. Five tester reports. Zero of the five Phase 6 release-mechanics prerequisites are
done. Most of the v2.0.0 milestone is shipped epics that only need closing.

**Shape of the remaining road.** (1) A handful of real defects from testers and the soak; (2) the
native/mobile lane, which has not shipped a beta and carries the Apple Tap to Pay paperwork;
(3) the 2.x docs lane (roadmap#512), which Paul called the most important part; (4) release
plumbing the plan lists for Phase 6 that nobody has started.
