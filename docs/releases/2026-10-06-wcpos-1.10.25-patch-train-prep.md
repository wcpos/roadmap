## B1 — draft release body, app v1.10.25

- **Till totals now match your store to the cent** on stores that round tax at subtotal level. With WooCommerce's "Round tax at subtotal level" setting on, a sale could come out one cent different from the store's own total (US stores with stacked state and county rates), or a fraction of a cent different in tax on tax-inclusive prices, and the checkout stopped with a totals-changed alarm. The till now rounds tax exactly the way WooCommerce does.
- **Adding a Bluetooth printer no longer crashes the app** on iPad and iPhone. The first printer scan after launch could run before Bluetooth was ready — longer still while the Bluetooth permission prompt was on screen — and the app closed. The scan now waits for the adapter; the error, if it ever recurs, is reported with a real message instead of "No error message".

### B2-long — the same list, GitHub form (reference; use for the app/electron bodies' plugin context if wanted, or append to the free GitHub release after publishing)

= 1.10.21 - 2026/10/06 =

- **Till totals now match your store to the cent** on stores that round tax at subtotal level. With WooCommerce's "Round tax at subtotal level" setting on, a sale could come out one cent different from the store's own total (US stores with stacked state and county rates), or a fraction of a cent different in tax on tax-inclusive prices, and the checkout stopped with a totals-changed alarm. The till now rounds tax exactly the way WooCommerce does.
- **Adding a Bluetooth printer no longer crashes the app** on iPad and iPhone. The first printer scan after launch could run before Bluetooth was ready — longer still while the Bluetooth permission prompt was on screen — and the app closed. The scan now waits for the adapter; the error, if it ever recurs, is reported with a real message instead of "No error message".
- **Cashiers can only edit customer accounts**, never staff accounts. Any account with a staff role, other cashiers included, is out of their reach through the POS and through WordPress's own user screens and API. A cashier who also holds the Shop manager role keeps that role's rights.
- **Changing a user's password signs them out of the POS on every device.** Logging out of WordPress in a browser ends the POS session in that browser only; the user's other tills stay signed in. A POS access token is only honoured while the session that issued it is still live.
- **Cashiers can edit and delete their own sales again** when their role lacks "edit others' orders". "Own" was decided from the order's WordPress author, which is never the cashier; the POS now treats the cashier the order is assigned to (the one shown on the order in WooCommerce admin) as its owner. Stores on the default Cashier role, which has that capability, see no change.
- **Payment gateways with no customer-facing title now show a name** in POS settings and at the till, falling back to the name WooCommerce shows on its own Payments screen, then to the gateway ID. A title you set for the POS still wins.
- **Fixes for stores using High-Performance Order Storage:** three things read order details from the wrong place — the cash payment details recorded with an order (amount tendered and change) were always empty, the tax-ID field suggested from your order history was never found, and fiscal submission status was invisible to anything reading the order.
- **Connecting the app to a store with no REST link on its front page works again.** A 1.10.0 regression. The app's fallback check of `/wp-json/` was refused by the browser's cross-origin rules and read as "Site does not seem to be a WordPress site".
- **A staging copy of your store no longer overwrites the live store in the app's site list.** A copy made from the live database carried the same site ID, so the app treated the two as one store: connecting the copy replaced the live store's name, address and plugin version on its card instead of adding a second one. A copy at a different address now gets its own ID. Because of this, a store moved to a new domain shows up in the app as a new site and needs to be connected once more.
- Added (developers): split-tender orders on `POST /wcpos/v2/push/orders` via the `_woocommerce_pos_payments` order meta (one receipt payment row per tender, change summed; tenders listed under the totals on the WooCommerce order screen), fee, shipping and custom lines on the same push, and `GET /wcpos/v2/status` advertising `order_create_v5`, `order_payments_list` and `products_id_fast_path` capabilities plus `wcpos_version`. (#2113, #2137, #2139)
- Added (developers): `GET /wcpos/v2/products?per_page=-1&_fields=id,date_modified_gmt,stock_quantity,stock_status` answers from one database query instead of hydrating every product. (#2113)


Pro long form: the #609 lead above, then its long explanation from the prep's A1 row (stuck hidden `no` field; `tax_status: ""` / `tax_class: "parent"` sent to the till; both now resolve from the parent as WooCommerce does).
