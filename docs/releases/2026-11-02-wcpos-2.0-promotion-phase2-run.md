## What the plan got wrong

- **The monorepo `main` push deploys `https://wcpos.expo.app`, the hosted production client, with `eas deploy --production`.** The plan's 2.4 only named the E2E. A 1.11 merchant on the hosted client would meet a 2.0 app that refuses their plugin, and the 2.0 plugin is a draft they cannot install. Phase 4 or GA is where that flip belongs unless Paul says the hosted client is not merchant-facing. The monorepo and electron merges are held on that question.
- **Nothing wrote cdn.wcpos.com's release-lane pointer.** The publisher only moves `lane:next`; the spec's tag-push workflow in wcpos/web-bundle did not exist, so `manifest?ref=2.0` answered `no_compatible_bundle` after the first tag. Built as web-bundle#27 (script + workflow + tests; refuses killed lanes and backward moves; serialised repo-wide).
- **dev-free strips `Authorization` and every `X-WCPOS-*` header** (its "hostile edge" nginx tier). The 2.0 app moved auth to the query lane but trusted the CORS floor for the protocol signal and withheld the `wcpos_protocol` twin, so every sync answered 426 and the POS never loaded. Fixed in the cut (`customHeadersReachServer`: the echo must report the probe's own `X-WCPOS` marker as received); the protocol-signal E2E spec mirrors it.
- **Five more prerelease compares** beyond the plan's two: free's drift scripts, electron's forge prerelease flag, the dev-box mu-plugin's version guard, and the TypeScript literal comparisons of a pinned `TRANSLATION_VERSION` in electron and the monorepo (plus a test). Memory `prerelease-version-compare-traps-2-0-beta`.
- **The cut moves on promotion day.** Three reports PRs merged into monorepo `next` after the cut; folded in, bundle republished and re-tagged twice. Post the frozen notice before cutting next time. The free and Pro merges also pushed new strings, so the translations repo cut `2026.11.3` mid-run and its bot pinned free (#2527), Pro (#752), monorepo (#3195) and electron (#574) `main`s to it; electron's pin conflicted with the cut's `2026.11.2` and was folded in. The promote cuts carried `2026.11.2`; the beta cut (Phase 4) re-pins whatever is current.
- **Electron's Phase 0 sync kept `next`'s older electron (43.7.3) over `main`'s 43.8.1**, which carried a security fix; dependency review caught it along with shell-quote, seroval, body-parser, follow-redirects, launch-editor, node-forge and undici, all overridden to their patched lines; zod and webpack-dev-middleware are allow-listed (dev tooling, fix is a major their consumers cannot take).
- **Promotion-base ratchets** red on the promotion PRs, as the plan said for REST lane coverage, plus two it did not name: the monorepo's i18n computed-key allowlist and Uniwind allowlist (both compare with `main`'s baseline). Free was admin-merged over its ratchet; the monorepo's two sit on its held PR.
- **`detailed-receipt` gallery template** changed on both lanes at registry version 2; bumped to 3 in the cut so 1.11 merchants' copies are told they are behind.
