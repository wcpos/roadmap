## B1 — app v1.10.26 release body (`app-notes.md`)

- **"Checkout failed" no longer appears for a sale the store did accept** on a slow connection. The till now waits up to 45 seconds for the store's answer to a Checkout or Save instead of 15, a request that never answers is cut off after 30 seconds and retried, and a sale pressed while an earlier one was still being sent is no longer held back for up to a minute. Pay stays disabled with a spinner while the till waits. (#2424)
- **Sales waiting behind an expired login are now announced.** When the store refuses the till's session, a banner in the header reads "N sales waiting to sync. Log in to continue." with a Log in button, and the "Please log in to continue" toast fires once when the hold starts. Until now the till retried every waiting sale once a minute, for days, without telling the cashier. It now probes the store once every 30 seconds, backing off to every 5 minutes; a fresh Checkout or Save press is still sent at once, and a successful push clears the hold. (#2425, #2430)
- **A delete at the till is only dropped from the queue once the store confirms it.** A hosting provider's challenge page answering "OK" used to count as the deletion going through, so the till forgot a delete the store never ran. The delete now stays queued and is retried. (#2426)
- **Opening an order's refunds while offline, or after the app was in the background, shows an offline card instead of "Couldn't load refunds"**, with the order's local refund summary and a Retry that also runs on its own when the app wakes. A login refresh that lands while the app is asleep now finishes on wake instead of failing the request. (#2427)
- **A corrupt entry in the diagnostic log no longer crashes the Logs screen on iPhone, iPad and Android.** The log store is disposable and now resets itself. (#2428)

### B2-long — the same list, GitHub form (`plugin-notes-long.md`)

- **Paying with a hosted gateway such as Dintero, Mollie, PayPal or Klarna no longer jumps the till to the receipt before the payment.** A payment gateway that sends the customer to its own hosted checkout (Dintero, Mollie, PayPal, Klarna and others) jumped the till to the receipt before a payment method was chosen, since 1.10.20. The fix for pay-later gateways shipped in that version applied the gateway's POS order status as soon as the gateway returned success, which a hosted gateway also does at the moment it redirects, before any money has moved; marking the order Completed there made Dintero bounce it to On hold ("missing a transaction ID"), and the till reads either as a finished sale. The status is now applied only when the gateway redirects to the order's own received page, the way a quote, invoice or purchase-order gateway does, and only for a gateway that does not declare WooCommerce's refund capability. A few refund-capable gateways (Mollie's Pay by Bank with its payment screen skipped, an async "received" result from a hosted provider) also return the customer to the received page while the money is still on its way, and nothing in that redirect tells them apart from a quote gateway; the refund capability does, because a gateway that can give money back is one that collects it. Hosted gateways are left open and settle from their callback as before. Merchants on 1.10.20–1.10.22 with such a gateway should update. (#2151)
- the five B1 bullets, long form, as above
- Developers: a failed POS session revoke (`DELETE /wcpos/v1/auth/sessions/<jti>` and `DELETE /wcpos/v1/auth/sessions`) now returns its intended HTTP status — 400 for missing parameters, 404 for a session already revoked, 500 for a revoke that failed — instead of HTTP 200 with `success: false`. The bodies are unchanged. (#2147)

## Risk read

- **Free #2151 is on the payment path at the till.** It narrows when `apply_unpaid_gateway_order_status()` fires (received-page redirect + no refund capability, down from any successful `process_payment()`), i.e. it moves hosted gateways back to the 1.10.19 behaviour and keeps Quotes-style gateways on the 1.10.20 behaviour. Cash/card (`payment_complete()` sets `date_paid`), BACS/cheque/COD (own status filters) and Quotes (redirects to `get_return_url()`) are unchanged by test and by live drive. Reviewed through CodeRabbit threads (fragment hand-off, `PHP_INT_MAX` priority, plain permalinks, explicit ports) each pinned by a test.
- **Monorepo #2424/#2425/#2426 are on the client write path.** All `door:two-way`, client-only, no stored format or migration; each PR body carries mutation checks. Trade-offs stated in the bodies: an unreachable store now shows "Checkout failed" after 45 s instead of 15; a cashier who logs back in waits for the next probe (≤5 min) for background rows, their own press is exempt; a store answering a delete with a non-JSON 2xx keeps the delete queued (the plugin always answers `{}`).
- **#2430 moves the OAuth login handling into a shared hook** (`useSessionLoginFlow`) — verbatim move, `auth-error-handler.test.tsx` unchanged and green. Not verified on a live till.
- **Pro and electron carry no code of their own** — Pro is a vendored-free rebuild, electron a re-pin.
- No DB schema, option or table changes in any repo this train.
