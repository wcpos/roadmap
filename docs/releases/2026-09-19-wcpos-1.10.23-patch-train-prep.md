## Draft release body — app v1.10.23

- **A till no longer loses live records during its startup tidy-up.** With the POS open twice on the same device, the tidy-up could mistake live records — including the sign-in row — for damaged ones and remove them. It now leaves alone anything it cannot fully account for.
- **A sale that could not be sent because the till was signed out now waits for sign-in instead of being given up on.** The cashier is told as soon as it happens, and the sale sends itself once the session is back.

Phone and tablet apps on the 1.10.18 store build receive this version as an over-the-air update: it downloads in the background and applies on the next cold start after that, with no store update needed. Older store versions must first update from the App Store or Google Play.

(OTA paragraph assumes `eas fingerprint:compare` matches for both platforms — confirm before dispatching.)

## Risk read

Per the batch rule, everything on `main` ships unless an item earns a hold. One item earns a flag, not a hold:

- **⚠️ Free's 31 commits are three architecture refactors, and they are the risk concentration of this train.** `Controller_Registry` is +393 new lines while `API.php` loses 173 and `Orders_Controller` loses 157; fifteen `API\V2\*` pass-through classes are deleted and become aliases, with the v2 service map now *derived* from v1 at runtime. If a route silently stops registering, the POS does not degrade — it breaks. Mitigations are real: a 566-line `Test_Controller_Registry`, a 458-line `Test_Order_Write_Parity`, and `Lane Coverage` green on the tip. Against that: it took **five successive review-fix rounds** (#2055, #2057, #2058, #2060 — namespace-stamping edge cases, the last one landing 05:10Z today), and all three refactors were Codex-delegated `codex/arch-*` branches, where hollow wiring is the known failure mode. My read is that it is shippable, but it is the thing to look at if 1.10.19 misbehaves — not the storage work.
- **Free's `PHP Tests` on tip `bcd428a0` was still `in_progress` at survey time.** It must be green before the free leg. Everything else on that tip is green (Lane Coverage, Release, OPFS Worker Drift, Hook Docs, Update POT).
- **Monorepo storage containment took ~10 Codex/CodeRabbit review rounds**, which reads worse than it is: the change is *containment* (refuse to drop a row it cannot account for), so its failure mode is leaving damage in place rather than destroying data. Fails safe.
- **Order write path:** three hydration regressions were found and fixed inside this cycle. The money path, so worth a live smoke on dev-next after the bundle tag — save an order, edit a line, remove a variation line.
- Electron's single-instance lock changes launch behaviour on every desktop till. Small and reviewed (Greptile follow-up for the hidden-window case), but user-visible on day one.
- Native E2E red, if it appears, is the usual non-blocking lane.
