## Why this train exists

A merchant (several Divi sites) reported 20–30 s from Pay to printed receipt on web and desktop, and desktop receipts that broke words mid-word and differed from web. Both devices use the same thermal template: the browser sends it raw to the printer, the desktop had no printer profile (profiles are per device) and silently printed the preview through the dialog. The preview's mid-word wrapping is fixed and the silent fallback now warns. The delay is partly addressed (auto-print no longer waits for the preview frame, the payment page signals before the theme's head, the Epson lane probes once); its full cause is still unproven and the notes do not claim it fixed. Added at the go: #2793, guest sales taxed at 0 on "tax based on shipping" stores (5 of 11 class B Sentry rows on 1.11.0–1.11.2).

## B1 — app notes (app 1.11.4 release body; desktop body = the same lines + footer, nothing is web-only this train)

- **Thermal receipts no longer break words in the middle** ("devolucione / s") when printed through the print dialog or as a full-receipt image. Text now wraps at word boundaries, as it does on a receipt printer. (#2804)
- **The receipt screen warns when what prints will differ from the template you chose.** A thermal template with no receipt printer prints its on-screen preview through the print dialog; an HTML template on a receipt printer prints the built-in layout; an HTML template on a Star CloudPRNT or Epson cloud printer prints nothing. The warning waits for the printer list to load, and it is translated. (#2808, #2813)
- **Automatic receipt printing no longer waits for the on-screen preview to load when the printer does not use it**: receipt printers, cloud printers and already-rendered templates. It does wait for the printer list, including a slow cloud-printer lookup, and for the store's receipt data or a settled order, so it never prints to the wrong printer or prints an "open" receipt. (#2806, #2814, #2815)
- **Card terminals and other non-cash gateways open the receipt as soon as the payment completes**, instead of waiting, sometimes indefinitely, for the order to sync. (#2807)
- **A receipt that could not be downloaded in time is reported as that**, not as a printer that did not answer. (#2790)
- **Desktop: Epson network printers on port 9100 start printing up to 16 s sooner.** The app checks the printer's other ports once per receipt instead of twice. (#2809; the figure is from the code, 4 ports × 4 s, not a measurement)
- **Guest sales on a store that taxes by shipping address are taxed at the till again.** With no shipping address the till now falls back to the billing address, then to the shop address, as WooCommerce does. These sales were taxed at 0 on the till and then refused at checkout because the store's total differed. (#2793)
- **The login screen says the app needs updating when the store's plugin no longer supports it**, instead of offering "Re-authenticate" and "No stores found". (#2792)
- **Logs: a catalogue change the store refused is always reported, and switching store mid-sync no longer leaves a row in the other store's log.** (#2787, #2788)
- Developers: translations updated to 2026.10.8.

Plugin-side lines (B2 only): thermal word wrap also as a PDF (free #2382); **Receipts open sooner after payment on sites with a heavy theme** (#2383); **Saving a product barcode, a cloud-print order, a negative fee's tax or a receipt template no longer saves the record twice** (#2380); translations 2026.10.8. The readme entry and the Pro CHANGELOG entry are on the release branches (C2, C3).
