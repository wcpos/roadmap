## B1 — app notes (app v1.11.7 release body; desktop body = the same lines + footer)

- **The Email Receipt dialog closes itself once the email is sent or queued.** On 1.11.6 it stayed open after Send and had to be closed by hand (reported on the Mac app). A rejected address, or a send the store did not accept, keeps it open with the address still in the field so it can be corrected. (#3113)
- **A customer created from the cart's "Add new customer" form attaches to the order with its tax IDs.** The order and its receipt carried no tax ID for a customer created at the till; only a customer picked from the list did, so the workaround was to remove and re-select the customer. (#3110)
- **A new tax-ID row starts on the type the customer's country makes likely, with that country filled in.** A Spanish store's cashier gets Spain NIF / ES on every new row instead of EU VAT with a blank country, and the type list puts the likely types first. Nothing to configure. (#3097)
- **A price or total of 1e21 or more reads as its digits.** Twenty-two 1s typed into the numpad displayed as 1.11; the stored value was right, the display was not. (#3082)
- Translations updated to 2026.10.9 (50 strings across 50 locales). (#3055)

No plugin update ships with this release; the app works with every 1.11.x plugin.
