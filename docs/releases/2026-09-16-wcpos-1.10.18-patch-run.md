## Release notes (app v1.10.18)

- **Product search no longer re-reads the whole catalogue on every keystroke.** Once the search index has found the matching products, the app now fetches only those records instead of scanning every product in storage twice. On large catalogues this was the fixed part of search latency that no index tuning could remove.
- **Typing in a search box with a keyboard-mode barcode scanner attached is now treated as typing.** With a search box focused, a keyboard-wedge scanner's keystrokes go into that box as text instead of being detected as a scan; a fast burst typed into the field could previously be replayed into search and the cart. Cashiers who click into the search box and then scan will notice this: scan with no text field focused instead.
- **Search boxes keep your cursor and text when the app resets them.** A reset after a successful scan no longer rebuilds the input, so focus stays put and half-typed text is not lost.
- **A Logs screen left open in the background no longer loads the whole log history.** A hidden Logs screen kept paging in every record until it held the full history (observed at 46,000 records and about 3 GB of memory on one till). Pagination now waits until the screen is actually visible.
- **Startup log cleanup no longer loads every log into memory.** The 30-day / 25 MiB retention pass read the entire log history at once; it now works through it in bounded pages.
- **Products hidden from the POS on the server are now cleaned out of the till automatically.** Tills that cached an online-only product before the server-side fix (WooCommerce POS plugin 1.10.14, woocommerce-pos#1990) now remove it on the scheduled cleanup, even while the server is under load, without a manual sync or reset.
