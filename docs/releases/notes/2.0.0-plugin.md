# WCPOS 2.0.0: plugin release notes (free readme entry and Pro CHANGELOG)

<!-- Built by concatenation from 2.0.0-app.md (B2): the same bold leads in the same order, short form, then the plugin-side bullets. Edit a lead in the app file first, then here. -->

WCPOS 2.0 gives the POS its own checkout: cash, split payments and card readers handled at the register itself, with a cash drawer that opens and closes with each shift. Around it, every screen is redrawn in a new design, and the till keeps taking cash when the connection drops.

## Highlights

- **A checkout that belongs to the POS.** Checkout now opens in a tender pane beside the cart, where the keypad is the screen, instead of sending every sale through WooCommerce's order-pay page. <!-- monorepo#1794 monorepo#1898 monorepo#1993 monorepo#1999 -->
- **Split payments.** Take one order in several payments, for example part cash and part card. <!-- monorepo#1929 monorepo#1997 monorepo#2456 -->
- **Card readers connected straight to the POS (Pro).** Stripe Terminal and SumUp readers pair with the till itself, over Bluetooth, and the POS shows each step of the card payment as it happens. <!-- monorepo#1956 monorepo#1961 monorepo#1964 monorepo#2393 monorepo#2419 -->
- **Tap to Pay on iPhone and Android.** With Stripe, the phone or tablet running WCPOS can take contactless cards itself, with no separate reader. <!-- monorepo#2405 monorepo#2419 monorepo#2507 monorepo#2537 --> <!-- CHECK -->
- **Registers and cash drawer sessions.** Each register can open and close a drawer session: an opening float, cash in and out during the shift, a count at close and the difference against what was expected. <!-- monorepo#1996 monorepo#2005 monorepo#3073 woocommerce-pos#1961 woocommerce-pos#2392 -->
- **Closures are saved, numbered documents.** Closing the register writes a numbered closure with the drawer count, tenders, taxes, discounts and items sold, which you can reprint, recount and export. <!-- monorepo#2131 monorepo#3048 monorepo#3058 monorepo#3105 woocommerce-pos#1962 woocommerce-pos#2484 -->
- **Cash sales keep going when the connection drops.** While offline, the till takes cash and records the sale, and asks the cashier to confirm first. <!-- monorepo#2865 monorepo#2870 -->
- **A new Reports screen.** Sales for any period with a comparison, a chart and cards for orders, top products, taxes, refunds, payments, categories and cashiers, plus cost, profit and margin. <!-- monorepo#2238 monorepo#2241 monorepo#2246 monorepo#2250 monorepo#2254 monorepo#2256 monorepo#2274 monorepo#3078 -->
- **A customer-facing display (Pro).** Open the display page on any device's browser, pair it to a till with a code, and it shows the cart and total as you ring the sale up. <!-- monorepo#1824 woocommerce-pos-pro#513 woocommerce-pos-pro#518 woocommerce-pos#1897 -->
- **A redesigned POS.** Every screen moves to a new design, with a Scale setting for compact, regular or spacious layouts. <!-- monorepo#2178 monorepo#2796 monorepo#3121 monorepo#3124 monorepo#3138 monorepo#3139 -->

## Breaking changes and requirements

- **WCPOS 2.0 needs WooCommerce 9.0 or later.** <!-- woocommerce-pos#1994 woocommerce-pos-pro#634 -->
- **The app and the plugin must both be on 2.0.** A 2.0 app will not work with a 1.11 plugin, and a 1.11 app asks for an update when it meets a 2.0 plugin. <!-- monorepo#2197 roadmap#195 --> <!-- CHECK -->
- **Sync every till before you upgrade.** 2.0 starts a new local database and does not carry over sales a 1.11 till has not sent yet. <!-- monorepo#3187 -->
- **Rolling back to 1.11 starts from an empty local database.** The till re-syncs from the store; anything 2.0 had not sent stays behind, so sync before you roll back too. <!-- monorepo#3187 -->
- **The experimental magnetic card reader is removed.** <!-- woocommerce-pos-pro#578 -->
- **Card terminal extensions need WCPOS Pro from 2.0.** This includes Stripe and SumUp. <!-- roadmap#95 -->
