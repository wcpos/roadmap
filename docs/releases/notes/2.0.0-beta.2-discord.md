# Discord announcement: WCPOS 2.0 public beta, beta 2

Paste everything below the line. Discord renders the bold and the bullets; keep each paragraph on one line.

📣 WCPOS 2.0 beta 2 is out.

Thank you for the first round of testing. Beta 2 carries the fixes and the two additions from it; it is still web and desktop only, and still a manual install: nothing is offered as an update, and the Android and iOS apps come out once beta testing is finished.

**Since beta 1**
• A Fonts setting under Settings > Theme: match the theme, or pick one of five type sets.
• The Free upgrade strip is the topmost row on every page, in the warning colour on every theme.
• Theme fixes: the cards under a stacked product tile take the theme's tile radius, and Market stall's primary colour is the coral from its design.
• The POS mark is redrawn: a slate wall behind a crimson and off-white awning.
• The POS page's splash matches the app's splash, so a phone's browser bar is light instead of black while the app loads.

**Coming from beta 1:** install beta 2 by hand over beta 1; a beta 1 till keeps its local data. **New testers:** sync every till before upgrading from 1.11, and read the beta 1 post above for the requirements.

**Get it**
• Step by step: https://docs.wcpos.com/getting-started/testing-the-beta
• Free plugin: https://github.com/wcpos/woocommerce-pos/releases/tag/v2.0.0-beta.2
• Pro plugin: sign in at https://wcpos.com/account/downloads and use the Beta card (active licence required)
• Desktop for macOS, Windows and Linux: https://github.com/wcpos/electron/releases/tag/v2.0.0-beta.2
• Card terminals (Pro): the beta 1 extension releases still apply; nothing changed in them.

**What changed, in full:** https://github.com/wcpos/monorepo/releases/tag/v2.0.0-beta.2 (the "Since beta 1" section is at the top).

**Found something?** Tell us in this channel, or open an issue at https://github.com/wcpos/roadmap/issues with the `beta` label. Say which part (plugin, desktop, browser), the version, what you did and what happened; an order number helps for anything involving a sale.
