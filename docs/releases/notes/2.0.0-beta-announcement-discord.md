# Discord announcement: the WCPOS 2.0 public beta (first post, links at beta 2)

Nothing had been posted when beta 2 shipped (2026-11-03), so the opening announcement points at beta 2. Paste everything below the line. Discord renders the bold and the bullets; every link is a bare URL because masked links are not guaranteed to render in a normal message. Keep each paragraph on one line.

---

📣 @everyone The WCPOS 2.0 public beta is open.

1.10 rebuilt the sync engine under the hood. 2.0 is the bigger change, and this time you can see it: the whole interface is redrawn for touch screens, with bigger targets, panels that slide in instead of pop-ups, a keypad that is the screen at checkout, and a scale setting for the size of your device. We want your feedback before it becomes the release.

**What's new**
• Checkout in the POS itself: a tender pane beside the cart, split payments across cash and cards, no more trip through the WooCommerce order-pay page.
• Registers and cash drawer sessions: open with a float, cash in and out, count at close, and a numbered closure you can reprint and export.
• Card readers paired straight to the till (Pro): Stripe Terminal and SumUp over Bluetooth, and Tap to Pay on iPhone and Android.
• Cash sales keep going when the connection drops.
• A new Reports screen, and a customer-facing display you open in any browser (Pro).
• Quick discounts, tax-exempt orders, quick filter buttons, browsing by category, in-place price and quantity editing, one print sheet for every document, and a Fonts setting to go with the themes.

**Just want a look?** https://demo.wcpos.com/pos is running the 2.0 beta; try it there without touching your own site.

**This is a beta: web and desktop only, and a manual install.** The Android and iOS apps come out once beta testing is finished. Run it on a staging copy of your store, or one you can afford to interrupt. It is never offered as an update; you download it, install it by hand, and do the same for each new beta build. WordPress.org and the app stores stay on 1.11 until the release.

**Sync every till before you install.** 2.0 starts a fresh local database and does not carry over sales a 1.11 till has not sent yet. You need WooCommerce 9.0 or later, and the app and the plugin must both be on 2.0, so keep phones and tablets on a 1.11 store until the mobile apps ship.
