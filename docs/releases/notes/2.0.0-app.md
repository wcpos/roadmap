# WCPOS 2.0.0: app release notes

WCPOS 2.0 gives the POS its own checkout: cash, split payments and card readers handled at the register itself, with a cash drawer that opens and closes with each shift. Around it, every screen is redrawn in a new design, and the till keeps taking cash when the connection drops.

## Highlights

- **A checkout that belongs to the POS.** Checkout now opens in a tender pane beside the cart, where the keypad is the screen, instead of sending every sale through WooCommerce's order-pay page. The old pay page is still there as a tab for gateways that need it. <!-- monorepo#1794 monorepo#1898 monorepo#1993 monorepo#1999 -->
- **Split payments.** Take one order in several payments, for example part cash and part card. The split is shown as a plan, with the payment you are taking now on its own line. <!-- monorepo#1929 monorepo#1997 monorepo#2456 -->
- **Card readers connected straight to the POS (Pro).** Stripe Terminal and SumUp readers pair with the till itself, over Bluetooth, and the POS shows each step of the card payment as it happens. A new Settings > Card readers page connects, updates, disconnects and forgets readers. <!-- monorepo#1956 monorepo#1961 monorepo#1964 monorepo#2393 monorepo#2419 -->
- **Tap to Pay on iPhone and Android.** With Stripe, the phone or tablet running WCPOS can take contactless cards itself, with no separate reader. An administrator sets it up once from Settings > Card readers. <!-- monorepo#2405 monorepo#2419 monorepo#2507 monorepo#2537 --> <!-- CHECK -->
- **Registers and cash drawer sessions.** Each register can open and close a drawer session: an opening float, cash in and out during the shift, a count at close and the difference against what was expected. Turn it on in POS settings; the register bar on the POS shows which till you are on and its session. <!-- monorepo#1996 monorepo#2005 monorepo#3073 woocommerce-pos#1961 woocommerce-pos#2392 -->
- **Closures are saved, numbered documents.** Closing the register writes a numbered closure with the drawer count, tenders, taxes, discounts and items sold, which you can reprint, recount and export. On Pro, a Closures room in Reports lists every session. <!-- monorepo#2131 monorepo#3048 monorepo#3058 monorepo#3105 woocommerce-pos#1962 woocommerce-pos#2484 -->
- **Cash sales keep going when the connection drops.** While offline, the till takes cash and records the sale, and asks the cashier to confirm first. The register bar, the receipt and the open-orders list all say "awaiting store" until the store has the sale. <!-- monorepo#2865 monorepo#2870 -->
- **A new Reports screen.** Sales for any period with a comparison, a chart and cards for orders, top products, taxes, refunds, payments, categories and cashiers, plus cost, profit and margin. Every report prints through a report template and exports to CSV. <!-- monorepo#2238 monorepo#2241 monorepo#2246 monorepo#2250 monorepo#2254 monorepo#2256 monorepo#2274 monorepo#3078 -->
- **A customer-facing display (Pro).** Open the display page on any device's browser, pair it to a till with a code, and it shows the cart and total as you ring the sale up. Pick a display design from the template gallery. <!-- monorepo#1824 woocommerce-pos-pro#513 woocommerce-pos-pro#518 woocommerce-pos#1897 -->
- **A redesigned POS.** Every screen moves to a new design, with a Scale setting for compact, regular or spacious layouts. Four personality themes (Paper till, Warm shop, Bold money and Market stall) set colours, type and shape, and you can choose an icon set. <!-- monorepo#2178 monorepo#2796 monorepo#3121 monorepo#3124 monorepo#3138 monorepo#3139 -->

## Breaking changes and requirements

- **WCPOS 2.0 needs WooCommerce 9.0 or later.** <!-- woocommerce-pos#1994 woocommerce-pos-pro#634 -->
- **The app and the plugin must both be on 2.0.** A 2.0 app will not work with a 1.11 plugin, and a 1.11 app asks for an update when it meets a 2.0 plugin. <!-- monorepo#2197 roadmap#195 --> <!-- CHECK -->
- **Sync every till before you upgrade.** 2.0 starts a new local database and does not carry over sales a 1.11 till has not sent yet. <!-- monorepo#3187 -->
- **Rolling back to 1.11 starts from an empty local database.** The till re-syncs from the store; anything 2.0 had not sent stays behind, so sync before you roll back too. <!-- monorepo#3187 -->
- **The experimental magnetic card reader is removed.** <!-- woocommerce-pos-pro#578 -->
- **Card terminal extensions need WCPOS Pro from 2.0.** This includes Stripe and SumUp. <!-- roadmap#95 -->
