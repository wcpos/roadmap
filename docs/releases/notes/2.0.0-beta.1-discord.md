📣 @everyone The WCPOS 2.0 public beta is open.

1.10 was a big update under the hood: the sync engine was replaced end to end. 2.0 is bigger still, and this time you will see it. There are new features, and the whole interface has been redrawn, because touch devices are the focus of the 2.0 cycle. A touch screen needs a different kind of UI from a mouse and keyboard: bigger targets, panels that slide in instead of pop-up windows, a keypad where the screen is the tender, and a scale setting (compact, regular, spacious) for the size of your device. It is a big change, and we want your feedback on it before it becomes the release.

**What's new**
• A checkout that belongs to the POS. Checkout opens in a tender pane beside the cart, with the keypad as the screen, instead of sending every sale through the WooCommerce order-pay page. Split a sale across cash and card, or between several cards.
• Registers and cash drawer sessions. Open the register with a float, record cash in and out during the shift, count the drawer at close and see the difference against what was expected. Each close is a numbered closure you can reprint, recount and export.
• Card readers connected straight to the POS (Pro). Stripe Terminal and SumUp readers pair with the till over Bluetooth, and Tap to Pay on iPhone and Android takes contactless cards with no separate reader.
• Cash sales keep going when the connection drops. The till takes cash and records the sale offline, and asks the cashier to confirm first.
• A new Reports screen. Sales for any period with a comparison, a chart and cards for orders, top products, taxes, refunds, payments, categories and cashiers.
• A customer-facing display (Pro). Open the display page in any device's browser, pair it to a till with a code, and it shows the cart and total as you ring the sale up.
• Quick discount at the till, tax-exempt orders, quick filter buttons, browsing by category, prices and quantities that edit in place with a keypad, one print sheet for every document, and receipts that carry the register, the software version and a real QR code.

**This is a beta, and it is web and desktop only for now.** The browser POS and the desktop app are in the beta; the Android and iOS apps come out once beta testing is finished. Run it on a staging copy of your store, or a store you can afford to interrupt. It is never offered as an update: you download it and install it by hand, and when beta 2 lands you update by hand again. WordPress.org and the app stores stay on 1.11 until the release.

**Before you install, sync every till.** 2.0 starts a fresh local database on each device and does not carry over sales a 1.11 till has not sent yet. Open each till on 1.11, let it finish syncing, then update. You need WooCommerce 9.0 or later, and the app and the plugin must both be on 2.0: a 1.11 app stops at an update notice when it meets a 2.0 store, so keep your phones and tablets on a 1.11 store until the mobile apps ship.
