Rules the mockup follows
One scan covers USB, paired Bluetooth and Wi-Fi at once; a plugged-in printer shows the moment it is found. Bluetooth LE needs a tap, so "Find a Bluetooth printer" stays in view on every scan screen, never under Options.

One large status line while the app works; the printer name becomes the headline once found; everything else is secondary.

Candidates are cards, the whole card is the tap target and selects; the test page is always its own tap ("Print a test page"), never automatic (Paul, 2026-09-05). Status is a coloured pill, the source a small tag.

The question has three full-width answers, the affirmative primary. The finish screen has one tick, one name, one sentence, one button.

Options is a quiet disclosure at the bottom, closed by default, fields in a two-column grid. No progress bar unless the scan runs long. The app never explains: one line, the actions, and a link to the printer guide on docs.wcpos.com.
