Phone: the menu in the search row, one row fewer than A/B — the whole grid row you see here is the gain. The list-view toggle folds into the sliders.

Recommendation: A for md, and A's phone. md is iPad portrait — a shop tablet on a stand, not a phone — and the rail is what makes Orders, Customers and Reports one tap away with no menu to open (rule 1: reachable in one or two taps, never hidden). It also removes the hamburger from every width but the phone, so there is one navigation model per device class instead of three. On the phone the bar over both tabs is what the (tabs) route already ships; the fix is to route into it. B is the fallback if the 48 pt rail proves too tight beside a 6-column keypad on an 8" Android tablet; C is the one I'd argue against — it scatters "where and who" between the two columns to save a row.

Under every option the resize bug goes: crossing into sm lands on the (tabs) route (and back out, on (columns)), so there is one phone UI. The columns route's own tab fallback ((pos)/(columns)/index.tsx line 47) is deleted — it was the register switch's stage-1 stand-in, and it keeps the scanner's "both panes stay mounted" rule only by being a second copy of the tabs.
