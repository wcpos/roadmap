Reports — second cut: the chart, the orders, the report

Prototype for wcpos/roadmap#304. After the first cut read as complex, this one is the app's own Reports page rehosted: the filter pills it already has, the chart it already has (now a comparison), the orders list with its checkboxes, and the report pane. The report picker is one select in the report card's header, the merchant's templates sit beside it, and sessions live inside the date pill. Catalogue from #303, contract from #305. The dark strip is not part of the design.

Where the register panel's X-report and last-closure reprint sit

The panel as decided on #214 (left) and what each of its two report rows does (right). The panel prints; Reports reads.

1
Print X-report prints the Session report for the open session on the till's printer, right there. It is a shortcut into the same document Reports shows when the date pill says Open now and the report is Session; it does not navigate. Free.
2
Last closure row — Reprint copy prints Closure 12 again (Free floor: reprint the last closure). Tapping the row opens Reports with the date pill on that closure and the report on Session. On Free that works while the closure is today's on this register; an older last closure still reprints from here but does not open on Reports.
3
Reports › Session carries the same two actions in its footer: Print X-report for the open session, Reprint copy for a closed one. Same document, same template, so a merchant who edits the closure template in WP Admin changes both.

What to react to

  

    
    
      
  1. The chart is the top of the page. One headline figure with a delta, three comparisons: today vs yesterday by hour, this week vs last by day, online vs POS by day. It follows the pills (register, cashier, store) and the ticked orders. On phone it keeps the figure and the bars, the comparison switch goes full-width.
    2. 
      
  2. One select for the report, in the report card's header, grouped: Sales summary and the Sales groupings, Cash movements, Session, Closures, then From plugins. An entry the current scope cannot answer is dimmed with the reason (pick a session / pick dates) rather than hidden.
    3. 
      
  3. Templates from WP Admin sit in a second select beside it, listing the merchant's own templates for the type the chosen report uses (closure for Session, report for the tables). The pane renders the chosen template, the way the Z-report pane renders today. Default is the built-in; Thermal 80 mm and A4 sales sheet are the merchant's. A template formats a report; it cannot add data — that is what a registered report is for.
    4. 
      
  4. Sessions live in the date pill. Its popover starts with Open now and today's closures, then the date presets. No separate mode switch: choosing a session scopes the page to that session; choosing dates scopes it to the range. Session and Closures dim in the report select according to which was chosen.
    5. 
      
  5. The orders list keeps its checkboxes. Unticking an order takes it out of the figure, the chart and the Sales report. It does not touch Session or Closures, which are records, not queries.
    6. 
      
  6. Free is unchanged from the first cut's decision: Pro rows in the date, register and store popovers are dimmed with a lock; tapping one names the tapped thing in the popover foot with See Pro. Nothing on the page is blurred.
