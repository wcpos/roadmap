What to react to

  1. Does the Sales room feel like a glance rather than a report builder? One number, its comparison, the hours, the tiles.
  2. Dates: the title is the menu. Today, Yesterday, This week, Last week, This month, Last month, and a custom range up to 3 months; ‹ › step by the unit chosen. Is the 3-month cap right, with longer runs pointed at WP Admin Analytics?
  3. Templates: one select in every panel header, listing the merchant's WP Admin templates for that type (report / closure); the pane renders in it and Print prints what you see. Try Weekend handover on Sales summary.
  4. The comparison is on by default and the referent is the same weekday last week (what Shopify, Toast and Square do). Yesterday and last year are one tap away. Right default?
  5. Ghost bars for the comparison period, including the hours still to come today. Helpful or noise?
  6. Tiles open the real table in a side panel with Export and Print. The print icon on the hero opens the Sales summary, the document the first cut led with. Is that deep enough for the "few clicks" reach?
  7. Orders is a tile; its panel has the checkboxes, and unticking shows as a chip on the hero. Is folding the checkboxes one level down acceptable?
  8. Closures is its own room, with the open session at the top. Does the split Sales / Closures read as "the numbers" vs "the paperwork"?
  9. Free: today only on this register, and the comparison still shows (assumption; the ghost needs last week's orders on the device). Stepping back or picking Week/Month names the thing in a small popover.
