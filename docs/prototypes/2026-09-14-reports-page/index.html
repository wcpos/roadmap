Reports — the picker, the scope bar, and the Free edge

Prototype for wcpos/roadmap#304. The catalogue is the four entries from #303 (Session · Closures · Sales · Cash movements) plus one registered report from a plugin (#305). A searchable list of reports, one scope bar shared by all of them, and a Free edition that never blurs the page: the scope is the gate. The dark strip is not part of the design; everything inside the frame is live.

The two shapes, side by side

Left: the Session report — a document, closure-shaped, rendered through the closure template; here Closure 11 as recorded, with its settled figures beside the recorded ones. Right: the Sales report by payment method — a table, rendered through the report template: columns, rows, totals; a grouping changes the columns, never the shape.

Where the register panel's X-report and last-closure reprint sit

The panel as decided on #214 (left) and what each of its two report rows does (right). The panel prints; Reports reads. Neither row leaves the till to print.

1
Print X-report prints the Session report for the open session on the till's printer, right there. It is a shortcut into the same document Reports › Session shows; it does not navigate. Free.
2
Last closure row — Reprint copy prints Closure 12 again (Free floor: reprint the last closure). Tapping the row itself opens Reports › Session on that closure. On Free that opens only when the closure is today's on this register; an older last closure still reprints from here but does not open on Reports — the one place the Free floor is wider than the Free page.
3
Reports › Session carries the same two actions in its footer: Print X-report for the open session, Reprint copy for a closed one. Same document, same template, so a merchant who edits the closure template changes both.
4
Nothing else from Reports appears in the panel. Sales, Cash movements and Closures are reading, not printing, and the panel stays the drawer.
