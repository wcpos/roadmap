## Patterns that recur (4+ apps agree)

1. **Merchant-chosen colour is the no-image fallback.** Square, Lightspeed, Loyverse, Zettle, SumUp, Toast, Clover, Revel; only Clover auto-assigns; Square adds a two-letter abbreviation. Shopify is the outlier (image-or-nothing).
2. **Image is the whole tile, text is a strip.** Shopify uses the image as the background; Square/Lightspeed/Odoo crop it on top with a name strip below; Toast and Lightspeed specify a landscape crop. Nobody renders a square thumbnail inside a padded card.
3. **Stock on the tile is a plain count, never a status phrase.** Square prints the number, Toast prints the remaining quantity, Clover/Toast grey-out plus "0" when gone; low-stock warnings are notifications, not tile badges.
4. **Variants: parent tile opens a chooser; "variation tiles" are an explicit, merchant-placed second tile type** (Square, Lightspeed). No app shows a "from" price range on the tile.
5. **Category/folder tiles are the same shape as product tiles, distinguished by behaviour and colour, not an icon.** Only Odoo moves categories out of the grid into a bar.
6. **Merchant-arranged pages, not auto-sort,** is the default across Square, Shopify, Lightspeed, Loyverse, Zettle, SumUp Pro.
7. **Nothing on the tile beyond name/price/stock.** None of the ten shows SKU, barcode, category list, tax line, cost of goods, or a sale strike-through on the grid tile.

## Distinctive ideas worth stealing

- **In-cart quantity printed on the card** as a large muted numeral — Odoo 18 `productCartQty` [41][42].
- **Category tint inherited by product cards** — Odoo colours a product by `product.color || last pos_categ.color` [42].
- **Variation tiles as a first-class type** — Square lets the merchant pin "Large / Red" so the tap skips the picker [1]; pair with the parent tile's summed stock [6].
- **Light/dark colour pairs** — Toast defines each button colour as a light-mode and dark-mode hex pair [32].
- **Two-letter abbreviation on colour tiles** — Square [3][4]; "IN/IN/IN" collisions are a known complaint [4].
- **Greyed + "0" corner badge for sold out, count otherwise** — Toast [31][34].
- **Unselected categories at 50 % opacity** — Odoo category bar [44].
- **"Modern vs Colored" style toggle and "hide product images"** — SumUp [25][27].
