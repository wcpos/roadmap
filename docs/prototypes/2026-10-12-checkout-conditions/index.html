Cart: a checkout condition is unmet (stock)

The question. Prevent overselling is on and stock moved while the cart sat open: a line now asks for more than the app knows exists. The tender-commit guard will refuse it anyway; this board is about what the cashier sees before tapping Pay, and how they reach the fix. Four structurally different answers on the real cart (the 2026-10-05 captures and the next source), in English.

Same in all four: the over-stock line is marked in place with the stock icon and the real string (2 available, Out of stock) and its quantity box turns attention-amber, because the fix is on the line. Today's n_available cell paints that line in text-destructive; the board moves it to the attention colour, since red on the till means void (rule 7). Messages reuse only_n_available ("Only 2 available for Belt"). The label follows the capture ("Pay"); the en string today is "Checkout {order_total}". After tap shows where each variant's tap lands: A, B and D open the line's quantity keypad; C shows the refusal.
