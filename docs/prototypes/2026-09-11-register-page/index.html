Decided on this cut (2026-09-11)

  • No "Signed out" state. The register is reached inside a signed-in app (web login at wcpos.com or the app's connect screen). A second sign-in was a Square/Shopify shape, not ours (#209, #217).
  • No "Register" menu item. Reverses the menu half of #207. Register is a state of the POS screen plus one panel; managers who want history have Reports.
  • Sessions off means the POS screen is exactly today's. Nothing appears anywhere.
  • Landing page after sign-in is POS, with the gate inline. Moot as a question.
  • Back to selling exists, because counting is a mode of the POS screen and leaving it must go somewhere.
  • Closing with unpaid carts open is allowed. Open carts are unsynced orders, not tendered money; they belong to whoever opens next.
  • First sign-in names the register on the POS screen, not on the store picker — a cashier choosing a store should not also be asked to name a till.
