The terminal moment: a log area that reads

From the first SumUp Solo run (2026-10-05): "The log also sucks. We are going to need a nice, consistent log area. Can you mock up some examples." Four options below, each the real terminal-leg-view.tsx rehosted in the state you saw (11,00 € declined on Paul Solo), then the recommended one in two more states and on a phone.

What every option changes, before the log itself
  • A red cross at the ring's size when the leg fails, where the spinner was. The Paid beat has a 96 pt tick; failure gets the same weight, red. A cancel or time-out gets a grey minus at the same size, not red: nobody lost money.
  • The failed step is marked, not left green. "On terminal" was shown green-and-bold after the decline as if still live.
  • Words, not wire. Reader action rdr_20XY…:4297… created on rdr_20XY… becomes Sent to Paul Solo; the ids move to a muted mono line only where the log is open. One message catalogue maps every server and client event to cashier copy; unknown messages fall through unchanged.
  • "Declined or cancelled on the terminal" when the provider cannot tell them apart (SumUp reports both as FAILED). "Cancelled on the terminal" after a declined card is wrong copy.
  • One row shape everywhere: level mark · message · time. Same row in the Logs screen, the register closure, the printer log.

Today what you saw, for reference

Green dot on the failed step, a monospace dump with reader ids twice per line, and a Copy button the size of a tile.

A · The steps are the log recommended

The four steps carry their own time and a one-line detail; anything that happens between steps (a cancel request, a wobble) attaches under the step it happened in. There is no second list. Details unfolds the ids and the copy action for support.

B · A proper list under the card

The step list stays as it is (with the cross). "Show log" opens a real list: level mark, words, time, muted ids on a second line, Copy in the list header. This is the Logs screen's row, so it is the cheapest consistent answer.

Declined, log open
Cheapest: a row component and the catalogue; the step list is untouched. Cost: two views of the same five events.

C · Status | Log inside the card

MoonPay's idiom: the reader card gets a segmented control. Status is the step list, Log is the row list, and the Log tab carries a red count when there is an error. Nothing below the buttons.

Declined, Log tab
Keeps the pane short and the card self-contained. Cost: a tab control at the moment the cashier least wants to explore.

D · A sheet

"Show log" opens a bottom sheet on a phone and a side panel on a tablet, with the list, the ids as key–value, and Copy / Share in its header. The pane behind never grows.

Tablet: side panel
Best for support calls (everything in one place, share from the header). Cost: a sheet primitive on both platforms; the log is one tap further away.
Phone: bottom sheet
