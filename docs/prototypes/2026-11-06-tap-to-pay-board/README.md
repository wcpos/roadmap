# Tap to Pay board (roadmap#113 slices 2 and 3)

Open `index.html` locally. `brief.md` is the brief, `rounds.md` the score tables and the pick, `variants/` the three
CSS-only pages (v1 picked, climbed through five Codex rounds; v2 and v3 as drawn in round 0) on the live tokens in
`variants/tokens.css`, `final/` the delivered v1 shots, `rounds/r0/shots/` the round-0 shots of all three, and
`rounds/r1..r5/codex.md` the judge output of each climb round (their shots were not kept).
