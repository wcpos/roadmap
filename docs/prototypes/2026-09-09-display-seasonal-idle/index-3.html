Customer display — three starting points for people who don't code

Each ships working with placeholder content and one obvious thing to change: the Carousel rotates three images (swap the three src lines for your own pictures; the caption is the line under each), the Specials board is a four-line list of items and prices, and Follow & review is your handle plus a QR image you paste in. All three carry a small version into the order screens: the carousel keeps cycling as a card under the totals, the specials add "Ask about today's specials", and follow adds your handle.
