What this applies from the design rules

  • Close, not a left arrow. The pane is on the left of the ledger; leaving checkout brings the products column back here. A left arrow pointed nowhere. Esc does the same on desktop.
  • One number. Total (or Remaining once a leg is taken) is the only large figure. A split or custom amount shows as a plan line under it, in the cashier's words, not as a second total.
  • A grid built for six gateways. Three fixed columns, every tile 96 pt tall, order set by the POS settings page and never reshuffled. Unavailable gateways stay where the cashier learned they are, dimmed, with the reason in one quiet line. Nothing orange unless the cashier can act on it.
  • Kinds are icons. Notes, card, terminal, Bluetooth reader. No OTHER label.
  • Touch sizes. Tiles 96, keys 56, Take 52, chips 40, close 44. Gaps 8–10.
  • State in one place. Offline is a badge under the total and the dot in the top bar; the tiles that need the store dim with Needs a connection.
  • Joy, defined. Pressed states in 100 ms; the change line rises as you type; pressing Take lands the payment in the ledger and stamps Paid when the balance reaches zero. Nothing else moves. Reduce-motion turns it all off.
  • Tokens, both themes. Colours are the semantic tokens from apps/main/global.css; blue primary, red only for Cancel payment. Dark mode is the same tokens swapped.
  • No paragraph. The tiles and the Split chip explain themselves.

Kept from the live screen

  • Payments / Legacy segmented control · Cancel payment once a leg exists · every disabled reason the code produces (no terminals set up, not available in the browser, needs a connection) · Works offline on Cash and Card · quick amounts and Full balance · terminal and reader lines with Change · Payment n of m · the ledger pane and cart tabs · phone: full-width pane with the ledger folded to a balance bar.
