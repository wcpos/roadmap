What changed against the live screen (all three)

  • One number leads. Remaining is the only large figure. This payment appears only when a split or custom amount makes it differ, and then it reads as a plan (Payment 1 of 2) not a second total.
  • “OTHER” is gone. The kind is an icon (notes, card, terminal, reader). A kind label repeats the title on five of seven tiles today and says nothing a cashier acts on.
  • Unavailable methods don't compete with live ones. Fresha and Square put a single explanation in one place instead of on each tile; here they fold into one Not available right now row (A, C) or the tail of the list (B), reasons kept.
  • Equal tiles, fixed grid. Tile width no longer follows the title, so the grid stops wrapping unevenly.
  • The paragraph at the bottom is gone. Split is a labelled button beside the amount; the tiles are self-describing. If a hint survives, it is one line under the tiles.
  • Offline is a state of the page, not of each tile. Offline puts one badge in the header and disables what needs the store; “Works offline” stays as a quiet badge on Cash and Card because it is the 1.11 promise.

A · Grid

The live layout, tidied: equal tiles, icon kinds, unavailable folded, keypad opens under the tiles as it does today. Least change, least risk.

B · List + panel

Methods as a list on the left (the Grab / Etsy / Wise idiom from Mobbin), a panel on the right that is the split plan until a method is tapped, then the keypad. Split leaves the header and becomes a real choice with amounts on it. Two orders in checkout still each get their own pane.

C · Two big buttons

Cash and Card carry the balance on them (Take 18,00 £) — the Toast / Odoo exact-amount shortcut from the vendor research. Terminals sit in a chip row beneath; card is one tap, cash opens the keypad because change is the question. Fastest for the 5 pm cashier, thinnest for a shop that lives on terminals.

Mobbin references pulled for this

  • Fresha · Select payment — four equal tiles with icons, one banner for the disabled tile, ledger and To pay on the right.
  • Square · Quick charge — Split payment as a secondary button beside the section title; one dismissible notice for what isn't set up.
  • Grab · Payment methods, Etsy · Choose a payment method, Wise · How would you like to pay — list rows, icon + title, one grey line for why an option is out.
  • World App · Buy, Monzo · Pay — amount leads, quick-amount chips above the keypad.

Questions this raises

  • C makes Card one tap for the exact balance. Is a confirm step wanted before a terminal starts, or is the terminal's own screen the confirm?
  • B moves Split out of the header into the panel. Is it still findable when a customer says “half on card” mid-sale?
  • Should “Works offline” show at all while online, or only once the till loses the store?
  • Where does Legacy go when a store has no webview gateway — hidden, or kept as a tab so the position never moves?
