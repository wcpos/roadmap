Direction 01, pushed

  • The amount is the screen. The balance is already typed when the pane opens, at 104 pt. Nothing to read, one thing to look at. Typing replaces it; the change line rises as you type; a short amount says how much is still to take.
  • The method is the commit. Cash and Card are white and carry the amount they will take. Terminals and readers sit beside them in the same row, in POS-settings order; six gateways wrap to a second row, they never hide. Unavailable ones are faded with one quiet line under the row saying why.
  • Card goes straight to the terminal moment. No confirm screen: the surface becomes the amount, the terminal's live steps and a way out, then the Paid moment. Cash goes to Paid at once.
  • Paid is a full-bleed beat. The surface turns green, the tick draws, the change is the headline for cash, then Print / Email / No receipt · New sale. This is the joy, and it is over in a second.
  • Split is a chooser with four modes, then a plan you can see. Even (2–6 ways), Amount (notes, Half, or type), Percent, Item (tick the lines this payment covers; the ledger then marks them paid). The strip under the number shows every leg: done ones green with their method, this one outlined, the rest dim; Change split re-plans the remainder at any time. The label and the commit button both say which payment this is and what is left.
  • Three surfaces to choose from: slate (the app's sidebar colour), the brand blue, and light. Same layout, same tokens.
  • Close, not an arrow. Esc on desktop.

Questions

  • Cash pre-typed to the balance means "exact" is one tap and "gave me 20" is two keys then a tap. Is that the right default, or should cash start empty so the cashier always types what was handed over?
  • Card as manual entry (no terminal) currently goes to Paid immediately. Should it ask for the last four, or a reference?
  • Terminal pills show the reader name only after they are the only one; should every terminal pill carry its reader name and battery?
