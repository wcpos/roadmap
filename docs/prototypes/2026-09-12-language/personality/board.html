Personality — six directions for the same register

Paul: "I like the design. I just find it uninspired, boring, dull." The language so far is the 2026 SaaS default: grey-blue neutrals, one blue, 8 px radius, the system font, hairlines, grey tiles, copy that says "Search products". Linear and Stripe look like that too, and they are only interesting because of what they add on top. Personality lives in five places this prototype has not touched: type with a voice, a canvas that is not white, the money as the hero, moments (what happens when a line lands or the sale is paid) and copy written by a person. Six directions below, each the same register, same data, same components. Only the tokens, the seams and the words move. References: Cash App's 44-point balance and green pay screen; Wise's lime and giant numerals; Uber Eats' black primary; Fresha's calm warmth; Linear's glow; the paper receipt every shop already prints.
