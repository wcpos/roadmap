What the money buys, 2026-09-16 Streamline (Plump, Flex, Freehand, Sharp, Core, Ultimate…): Icons plan from $19/month billed annually, 300,000+ icons across 55 sets, up to 100 icons per project on the standard licence; downloaded assets stay usable after cancelling. Plump and Freehand are the ones with a face; the free subsets above are 1,000–1,500 of each. The 100-per-project cap fits a POS (we use ~40). Hugeicons Pro: from $99/year (Untitled UI's 2026 review; the vendor page did not load), 60,000+ icons in 10 styles, Figma + SVG + React. The free stroke set is 6,000 and MIT. Rounded and friendly by default; the twotone and duotone styles are where the personality is. Nucleo: one-time, $149 for all 44,282 icons (Core, UI, Sharp, Pixel, Micro Bold), $99 per family, lifetime updates, Mac/Windows app. 18 px grid; precise rather than warm. Team plan $299/year. Untitled UI Icons: from $59 one-time, 4,600 icons, 4 styles, unlimited projects. Clean, neutral; not a personality buy. Free with character: Phosphor (MIT, six weights, duotone is quietly distinctive), Solar (CC BY, six styles, soft), MingCute (Apache, cute), Iconoir (MIT, thin and generous). Custom-drawn: ~40 icons at a freelance illustrator's rate is roughly $1,500–4,000 and 2–3 weeks, and it is the only route to a set that is nobody else's. A hybrid is common: buy a family for the 300 long-tail glyphs, commission the 15 that carry the brand (register, drawer, receipt, void, paid, the empty-state doodle).
