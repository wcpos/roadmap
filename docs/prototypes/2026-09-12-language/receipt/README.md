# Receipt in the register — roadmap#288

[Open the paid stage](index.html). The strip is not part of the design.

The paid stage replaces products, with the cart beside it; on the phone it fills the checkout
surface. Top to bottom: the tick and existing stamp, change or paid amount, settled payment
methods, the receipt's Template and Printer controls, a muted zoomable viewport holding an
80 mm paper drawing, then Next sale, Print, Email, PDF and the quiet No receipt · Next sale.
Syncing, offline, unavailable preview and captured-but-unfinished each have a state. The captured
warning keeps its support details and help link. Selecting Print dialog with the thermal template
reveals the amber mismatch; Invoice with Epson draws the other mismatch. Email is a centred
dialog at every width, with the billing-address switch and the offline queue wording. Orders →
an open order → Print receipt opens the same document body in its right-hand pane, with
Close · Email · PDF · Print and no automatic print.

## Decisions for Paul

1. **Auto-print on, with Print spinning** (drawn), or off until pressed (today's default).
   On removes a tap at every sale and says where the receipt went; off avoids unwanted paper.
   Both finish actions stay disabled through dispatch. **Pick: on** — the direction's
   “Print → no dialog, the receipt goes.” Printed means dispatched, not confirmed paper delivery.
2. **Draw the receipt paper** (drawn), or a blank preview placeholder. Paper makes the amount of
   chrome and the viewport's proportions judgeable; a placeholder makes no promises about a
   merchant's template. **Pick: drawn paper** — an 80 mm shape, not a new receipt template.
   Server-owned layouts, logos, fiscal fields and live content are not specified here.
3. **Next sale first, then Print · Email · PDF, with No receipt quiet** (drawn), or today's
   New sale · Email · PDF · Print order. The drawn order keeps the next customer first and paper
   closest among the secondary actions; today's keeps the learned secondary order. **Pick: drawn.**
   Print and Email do not finish a sale. No receipt returns to selling; it does not cancel a
   print that has already dispatched.
4. **Email in a centred dialog** (drawn, also today's app), or the side panel. Centre keeps the
   two-field task separate and lets the receipt stay underneath; a side panel matches the
   larger editors but hides more work. **Pick: centred**, including on the phone. Focus goes to
   the address; Enter submits a valid address; Escape or Cancel returns to Email on the receipt.

## Prototype boundaries / behavior changes

- Docs only. Print dispatch, email sending/queueing and PDF completion are local visual beats;
  no hardware, durable queue, download or server request is made. Email/PDF completion says demo.
- Amounts follow the current register cart, rather than the brief's illustrative £18 sale.
  `paid-change` adds £2.40 change; the ledger and paper use the same payments. The Orders receipt
  scene uses #102476 (£22.60). Other order fixtures aggregate unitemised balance as Other order items.
- Automatic print, the expanded stage, secondary action order and centred receipt email are the
  receipt drawing's changes; no app behavior is changed. The existing new-sale tear stays in use.
- Server-rendered templates, actual device printing/sharing and native Back/haptics are not
  evaluated by this browser drawing. Full-shoot verification is reserved for the combined run.
