Personality — the ideas, and what is live

Paul: "Not just a skin of what is there, but subtle changes that give this app its own personality." Research: Cash App (the number is the hero, and it moves), Wise (giant numerals, one loud colour), Duolingo (a character and a voice, but restraint is what makes the copy land), Tally, Typeform and Cake (one hand-drawn line for every empty state), Linear and Gmail (undo instead of dialogs), the paper receipt every shop prints. Every fintech on Mobbin celebrates with confetti; that is the one thing to avoid. The through-line for WCPOS: it is a till, run by a person.
