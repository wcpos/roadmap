Personality — the ideas, and what is live

Paul: "Not just a skin of what is there, but subtle changes that give this app its own personality." Research: Cash App (the number is the hero, and it moves), Wise (giant numerals, one loud colour), Duolingo (a character and a voice, but restraint is what makes the copy land), Tally, Typeform and Cake (one hand-drawn line for every empty state), Linear and Gmail (undo instead of dialogs), the paper receipt every shop prints. Every fintech on Mobbin celebrates with confetti; that is the one thing to avoid. The through-line for WCPOS: it is a till, run by a person. Seven touches are live in the register under Touches, each on its own toggle; the rest are listed for later.
live · total settlesWas a count-up; Paul: it would get boring. Now one 220 ms nudge of the total and the Checkout amount when a line lands, and nothing spins.
live · PAID stampNo confetti. A rubber stamp slams onto the paid screen at 8°, with a little ink fade. The one moment of theatre in the app, and it is the shop's own. Airline boarding passes, Notion's Done.
out · a voicePaul: the plain labels are safer with translations. Removed.
live · price-tag pencePence small and raised, £2410. Whimsical themes only (Market Stall, Paper till); the default themes keep plain numbers.
live · tile badgePaul did not want sold-today, but kept the badge: it now shows a property chosen in POS Products settings (stock for now, amber when low). Could be sale price or category instead.
live · receipt tears offAfter a sale, the cart column tears along a perforated edge and drops away; the fresh one rolls down. Whimsical themes only.
live · doodleOne hand-drawn line of a till with a curl of receipt for the empty cart; the same family would serve closed, offline and nothing found. The only illustration in the app. Tally, Typeform, Cake.
next · the cashier's dayThe user sheet and the close screen say how the day is going: 42 sales · £612 · busiest 12–1, with a tiny hour sparkline; "First one. Have a good day." on the first sale. Cash App earnings, Duolingo's restraint.
next · undo, never dialogsEvery destructive action is a toast with Undo: void, remove line, close. A trait, not a feature: the till trusts you. Gmail, Linear.
next · the scanner as a characterA beep, a green flash on the search field, and the scanned line drops into the cart with a little more weight than a tap.
next · time of day"Morning, Sam" on the open screen; the canvas warms after six. A shop that notices the hour. Things, Apple Weather.
next · the receipt the customer keepsThe printed and emailed receipt designed like the app: logo, "served by Sam", a thank-you line the owner writes, the doodle. Personality that leaves the shop with the customer.
next · sounds, opt-inA real ka-ching on Paid and a soft tick per line; off by default on desktop, a setting on tablets.
next · physical feedbackHold a tile for a quantity ring; the drawer glyph actually opens when the drawer opens; haptics on touch.
