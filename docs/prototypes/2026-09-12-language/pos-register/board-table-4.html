POS table — the filter bar, and the table slides for variations and categories

Both frames are live. Filter bar under the search, the app's own pills (Stock, Featured, On sale, Category, Tag, Brand) plus a user's quick filter and +. Variations: press a variable product (Tote bag, Tea tin) and the table slides left, a breadcrumb takes the top, the whole table is now the variations. Categories: press a category chip in a row, or pick one from the Category pill, and the same slide shows only that category. Products in the breadcrumb slides back. Pressing + on a variation adds it to the cart. Two surfaces, the two Paul is between: today's, and the floating sheet.
