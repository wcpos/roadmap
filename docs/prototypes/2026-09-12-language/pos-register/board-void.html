Round four — the end of the sale, re-imagined

Paul: "the problem is not whether the button has a border or not, it's far more fundamental than that." Agreed. Today's footer is a dialog's button row (Cancel | OK) put under a cart, so Void and Checkout read as peers, and every round so far restyled the wrong half of that pair. What the references do instead: the money and the sale's fate are two jobs and live in two places. Fresha (the one real POS on Mobbin with this footer): a To pay row, one full-width Pay now, a quiet ⋮ for everything else. Shopify treats the draft order as a document: Discard and Save sit in its title bar, nothing destructive at the foot. Glovo, UNIQLO, 7-Eleven: the footer is a bar, the total is the biggest thing on it, one action beside it. Lightspeed: park and discard are quiet text above Pay.
