POS — five ways to have no Void button

What the best products do about discarding a draft: Stripe's invoice editor has no delete on the page at all; Shopify shows Discard only while there are unsaved changes and puts cancellation under the order's own actions; Linear deletes from the item's ⋯ with an Undo toast; Apple swipes the row and keeps a Recently Deleted; browsers close the tab.
