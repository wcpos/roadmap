Cart line — quantity, edit and remove, five ways

Decided (Paul): the one-tap quick edit of name and price stays. The quantity is the button: press it and the box expands in place, the number exactly where it was, − to its left, + to its right, a keypad beneath, as if the border grew. On a phone it is a bottom sheet instead. Edit and delete live under the line: press and swipe the total column left; a short swipe snaps to Edit / Delete, a full swipe deletes; on a mouse, hovering the total nudges the line to show there is something under it, and a click on the total opens it. Below the fold, the five ideas that led here. Paul then: the stepper is right for cart quantity but takes too much room, so it should only be active on the active line; the swipe is intuitive on a tablet, but a row that slides on mouse-over would confuse desktop users, who still need two things on every line: edit (the line's details) and remove. Five answers, each drawn for touch and for mouse. The Pointer switch shows both; the same code runs, the reveal changes.
