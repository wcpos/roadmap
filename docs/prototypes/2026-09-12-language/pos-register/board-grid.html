POS grid — the drill-in transition, six ways

The table slides left behind a breadcrumb. The grid needs the same breadcrumb and a motion of its own, so this is what Mobbin shows for a grid that drills in. Push is what every iOS catalogue does (Afterpay, Shop, DICK'S, SKIMS): the grid moves left, the name becomes the title, a back arrow returns. Fade is what the web tools do (Craft, Air, Savee): the folder's contents replace the grid in place under a crumb, with no motion or a dissolve. Zoom is the media idiom (Apple Photos, Pinterest): the tapped tile opens out into the new view. Hero is Material's container transform: the tile flies up to become the breadcrumb chip while its children cascade in. Stagger is the same swap with the tiles arriving in sequence. Back tile is what Loyverse and Square do inside a POS grid: no breadcrumb row, the first tile of the child grid is the way back. Not on the board: Blinkit and Square Go, where category chips re-filter the grid in place and nothing navigates, which is what the filter pills already do. Every frame is live: tap Tote bag, or Category and a pick; the breadcrumb (or the back tile) returns. Tap anything else and it counts into the cart.
